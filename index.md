# RecipeMAGIC! — Support

RecipeMAGIC! is a recipe app for family and friends; accounts are by invitation.

**Need help, or want to be invited?** Email [jeff@thoseandersons.ca](mailto:jeff@thoseandersons.ca).

- [Privacy policy](privacy.html)

## Common questions

- **I can't sign in.** Check the email address you were invited with, or ask for a new invitation.
- **An import didn't work.** Open the recipe and tap Try again. If it still fails, email the link and I'll look.
- **Delete my account.** In the app: Account → Delete account. This removes your account, recipes and uploaded photos.
