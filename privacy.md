# RecipeMAGIC! Privacy Policy

**Effective:** October 19, 2026 · **Contact:** jeff@thoseandersons.ca

RecipeMAGIC! is a recipe app for a small group of family and friends. You get an account by invitation. This page says what the app keeps, where, and who else sees it. We do not run ads, we do not track you across other apps or sites, and the app has no analytics or advertising code.

## What we keep, and why

- **Your account:** your email address and a password. The password is kept only as a hash by our sign-in service (Supabase); we cannot read it.
- **Your recipes:** titles, ingredients, steps, notes, tags, and the photos, PDFs and links you add. We keep them so they are on every device you sign in on, and so you can share a recipe with a friend.
- **Import logs:** when you import a recipe from a link, our server writes a log line with the link as you sent it, whether it worked, how long it took, and technical detail about how the page was read. We use it to find and fix import problems. When you delete your account the log line is kept but is no longer tied to you; it still holds the link text.
- **On your device only:** a copy of your library so the app works offline, your cooking timers, and your settings. If you use Siri, Shortcuts or Spotlight, the app also keeps your recipe titles and IDs on the device so they can be found by name. **These titles stay on your device.** They are removed when you sign out. (Siri itself is Apple's service; what you say to Siri is handled by Apple under Apple's policy.)

## Who else handles it

- **Supabase** stores the account, recipes and files, and sends them back to your devices.
- **AI model providers (Anthropic, and OpenRouter which routes to other model makers):** when you import from a photo, a PDF, pasted text, or a web page or video that has no ready-made recipe data, the text or image of that content is sent to one of them to be read into a recipe. It is sent for that request; we do not send your account details with it.
- **Page and video readers:** to fetch a link you import we may use a page-reading service (Jina Reader, ScraperAPI) and, for videos, YouTube's and TikTok's own pages and a transcript service (Supadata). They see the link, not who you are.
- **Expo (EAS Update)** delivers app updates over the air. It sees that a device of this app version asked for an update, as any download service sees a request.
- **Apple** handles voice commands in Cook Mode. By default the phone recognises speech on the device. If you choose "use Apple's servers" when the app asks, your speech is sent to Apple for that. Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight are Apple's.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising.

## Your choices

- **Delete your account** in the app (Account → Delete account). That removes your account, recipes and the photos you uploaded. Recipes you shared with a friend are the friend's copy.
- **Export** your library from Account → Export library.
- **Trash** keeps deleted recipes until you empty it.
- **Ask us** at jeff@thoseandersons.ca to see or remove anything else.

## Children

The app is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

If this changes we will update this page and the date above.
